## Iterations (arms `<A|T>_16_s<seed>_kscr<n>`)

- **Iteration 1** is the proposal's from-scratch recipe on the knee alone:
  - `seq_adapt_2d`, K=16, 100 epochs, base schedule (step at 90).
  - Uniform start, knee PD + PDFS, R=8, 320², VarNet.
  - Three runs: A_16_s42_kscr1, T_16_s42_kscr1 and a floor twin T_16_s142_kscr1.
  - A and T start bitwise identical (same seed) and differ only in whether the sampler's residual sees the data.
- **Steering signal.** A from-scratch A − T gap is mostly training noise: seed spread in joint LOUPE+VarNet training is
  0.3–0.5 dB, and the scratch floor is unmeasured until the floor twin runs. So iterations are judged on the dev half by
  readouts with no training noise in them:
  - the trajectory swap cost;
  - per-step data-swap movement.

  The fine-tunes already reach a swap cost of +0.03 to +0.05 dB (A_1 +0.05), so "adapts more" means clearly above
  that level, not the proposal's +0.05 bar. A − T on dev and the floor T_s42 − T_s142 are reported beside them, but no
  iteration is chosen by A − T.
- **Who decides.** After each iteration the results go to the user, who picks the next change. Each new recipe gets a
  new `kscr<n>` in `common.sh`. Iterations are not chained automatically.

- **The bar is the proposal's:** G_16 ≥ +0.10 dB pooled in both seeds, each CI above 0, and at least twice the measured
