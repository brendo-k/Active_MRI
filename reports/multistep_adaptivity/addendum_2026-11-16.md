# Addendum 2026-11-16: kscr3, SeqMRI's own design on multi-coil knee

## Why

kscr2's SeqMRI-style arm (S_16) tied the adaptive arm: S − A was +0.001 dB. It differed from SeqMRI in many ways:
- 16 steps, with reconstructions before only 4 of them;
- a bounded residual on static LOUPE logits instead of a full sampler map;
- a sigmoid;
- the reconstruction route to earlier draws cut (report section 12, `kscr2/README.md`).

On 11-16 the user asked for a run that is SeqMRI apart from the multi-coil data and the VarNet. It should use SeqMRI's 4
steps and softplus, and reconstruct after every step. It trains on the lab server's GPUs 0–1, on 20% of the data, with
a 4-cascade VarNet. The user chose the data subset, the resolution, the recipe and the arms below.

## Arms

All arms use seed 42, so the VarNet and the sensitivity net start identical and see the same batch order.

| Arm | Sampler | Steps | VarNet passes per forward |
|---|---|---|---|
| F_4_s42_kscr3 | `seqmri_2d`: SeqMRI's Sampler2D (64 channels, 4 pools), softplus ÷ its max × (1 − M) | 4 | 5 |
| L_1_s42_kscr3 | `loupe_2d`: static LOUPE from uniform noise, one map for both conditions | 1 | 1 |
| Lc_1_s42_kscr3 | `loupe_2d` with one map per condition (`anatomy_conditioning=true`), like kscr2's T_1 | 1 | 1 |

Lc is an addition by the agent, not the user, and the user can drop it.
- F reads the data, so it can learn a different density for PD and PDFS; L cannot.
- Per-condition masks have been worth 0.1–0.25 dB in earlier rounds, so F − L could come out positive from contrast
  routing alone.
- Lc runs on GPU 1 after L, so it adds no wall time.

## What F takes from SeqMRI (its code is this repo's root commit cf503ae)

- **Reconstruction schedule:** the reconstructor runs on the centre, then after each step, with the same weights.
- **Sampler input:** the real and imaginary parts of the reconstruction's k-space, then those of the measured k-space,
  then the mask.
- **Mask draw:** the scores are rescaled to the step's budget and drawn by the straight-through threshold.
- **Gradients:** the union is detached between steps and the reconstructions are not. So an earlier step's draws reach
  the loss only through the reconstructions later steps read, and only the last step's draws reach it directly.
- **Loss:** on the final reconstruction only.
- **Centre:** 1/8 of the budget, 40×40 at 320² (SeqMRI used 16×16 at 128²). Both arms get it, as SeqMRI's LOUPE did.
- **No condition label:** SeqMRI used none, and its LOUPE had one map.

## What differs from SeqMRI, by the user's choices

- **Data:** multi-coil knee (15 coils) at 320×320, not single-coil cropped to 128×128.
  - The coils enter the sampler as the first ACS virtual coil, so measured values are kept and the rest stay 0, as in
    SeqMRI's input.
- **Reconstructor:** a 4-cascade VarNet with learned sensitivity maps, with cascade checkpointing so five passes fit
  in 48 GB. Both arms use the checkpointing.
- **Recipe:** kscr2's, not SeqMRI's.
  - kscr2: L1 loss, Muon at lr 1e-3 (the sampler and the LOUPE logits are in Adam at 1e-3), 100 epochs, ×0.1 at
    epoch 90, batch 16.
  - SeqMRI: SSIM loss, Adam at 5e-5, halved every 10 epochs, 50 epochs.
- **Training slices:** every 5th of the 80 local volumes, 544 of 2,718 (`datasets.train_slice_stride=5`).
  - kscr1 and kscr2 trained on all 2,718. Earlier notes said 1,447, but that was the manifest's cache plan, not the
    loader.
- **Arms:** two only. There is no data-blind twin and no sigmoid arm.
  - So F − L mixes the spacing gained by taking 4 steps with adaptivity, as SeqMRI's own comparison with LOUPE did.
  - At 16 steps, kscr2 split its +0.10 into +0.044 spacing and +0.059 adaptivity.

## Readouts

Scored on the dev half: 669 slices, draw seeds 1234 and 4321, as in kscr1 and kscr2.

- **Primary:** F − L, paired per slice. Pooled PSNR with a volume-cluster CI, and by condition.
- **Beside it:** F − Lc and Lc − L, which give the routing part.
- **Also, descriptive only:**
  - SSIM and NMSE;
  - F's swap cost through its own VarNet;
  - F's per-step data-swap movement.
  - The leak check showed that own-VarNet swap costs include co-adaptation.
- **Guards:**
  - pooled SSIM ≥ 0.80;
  - realised fraction 0.125 ± 0.002;
  - no failed reconstructions (below 20 dB).

## How the results will be read (fixed before the runs)

| F − L, pooled | Reading | Next, the user's call |
|---|---|---|
| CI above 0 | SeqMRI's design beats one-step LOUPE here; without a twin this cannot say how much is adaptivity | its data-blind twin, a second seed |
| CI includes 0 | no gap at this data size | the data-blind twin, or more data |
| CI below 0 | SeqMRI's design loses to LOUPE here, e.g. its U-Net cannot learn a density as good as LOUPE's free logits from 544 slices | none from this design |

- **Routing:** if F − L is above 0 but F − Lc's CI includes 0, the gap is contrast routing, not sequential sampling.
  Routing is roadmap step 1, which already has its own experiments.
- **Pilot success:** F − L pooled ≥ +0.10 dB, with the CI above 0 and the same sign on knee-PD and knee-PDFS. That
  matches kscr2's A − T_1 at a fifth of the data.
- **Inconclusive:** anything else short of the CI below 0.
- **Scope:** one seed cannot meet the experiment's seed criterion. A pass earns the twin and a second seed, not a claim.

## Expected outcome

- **For F:**
  - SeqMRI's 4-step model beat LOUPE by 0.47 SSIM points at 8× (single-coil, U-Net).
  - kscr2's 16-step arms beat one-step LOUPE by +0.10 dB.
- **Against F:**
  - Our one-step conv_2d sampler, also a U-Net that outputs the whole map, trailed LOUPE by 0.20–0.27 dB at R=8. Its
    mask was smooth and near-static.
  - F's sampler starts with no static map and learns one from 544 slices.
- **Guess:** F − L between −0.15 and +0.15 dB.

## Compute

- Lab server: F on GPU 0; L, then Lc, on GPU 1. Both queues start together, via
  `scripts/launch/train_multistep_kscr3_local.sh`.
- The smoke run (10% of one epoch) peaked at 34.0 GiB for F and 33.8 GiB for L at batch 16.
- Wall time is rough: about 8–11 h for F (five VarNet passes) and about 2 h each for L and Lc, then about 15 min each
  to score.
- Crashed runs are rerun in full.
